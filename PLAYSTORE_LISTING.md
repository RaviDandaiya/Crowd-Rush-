🔥 **GET READY FOR THE ULTIMATE MOB RUNNER THRILL!** 🔥

Welcome to **Crowd Rush: Mob Control 3D**, the most dynamic and hyper-casual crowd-building action runner on mobile! Choose your path wisely, smash through math multiplier gates, build a massive army of unstoppable runners, and obliterate enemy fortresses!

Can you master the multipliers and command the biggest crowd ever seen?

---

### 💥 EPIC GAMEPLAY FEATURES

👥 **MULTIPLY YOUR HORDE**
Steer through blue multiplier gates (×2, ×3, ×5, +50) to instantly multiply your squad from a small crew into a giant stampede! Beware of red dividing and subtracting gates that test your reflexes!

👑 **SPAWN GIANT CHAMPIONS**
Transform your crowd with towering Champion Giants! Crush through spinning saws, smash iron obstacle hammers, and steamroll enemy hordes without losing a single runner!

🏰 **CONQUER DESTRUCTIBLE FORTRESSES**
Every level climaxes with an explosive siege! Unleash your massive army against heavily fortified boss keeps, castles, and towers. Watch bricks and walls crumble into rubble under sheer crowd power!

⚡ **HYPER FEVER RUSH MODE**
Fill your Fever Gauge by hitting multiplier combos. Trigger Hyper Fever to become 100% invincible, blasting forward at supersonic speed with blazing rainbow trails!

🛡️ **POWER-UPS & BUFFS**
- **Energy Shield**: Protect your horde from hazards for 6 seconds.
- **Coin Magnet**: Vacuum every coin along the asphalt track.
- **Golden Multipliers**: Huge burst of bonus runners!

🎨 **CUSTOMIZE YOUR ARMY**
Earn coins and unlock 12+ legendary skins in the Shop:
- Classic Neon Blue & Steve
- Explosive Creeper & Crimson Devil
- Cyber Ninja, Superhero & Golden King
- 💀 Death Knight, ⚡ Thunder God & 🌌 Void Reaper!

🗺️ **30+ LEVELS & UNIQUE WORLDS**
Journey across vibrant 3D environments:

Log in every day to play dynamically generated Daily Levels for exclusive coin payouts and mystery skins!
