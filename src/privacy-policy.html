Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Crowd Rush: Mob Control 3D

This Privacy Policy describes how Crowd Rush ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and discloses information when you play our mobile application and web game.

1. Information We Collect

Crowd Rush is designed with user privacy as a primary principle. We do not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, address, or email address) to play the game.

Key Privacy Guarantee: All gameplay progress, high scores, coins, unlocked skins, and settings are saved locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the Game free to play, we integrate third-party advertising services (such as Unity Ads) to serve rewarded and interstitial ads. These ad providers may collect anonymous, non-personal device information, including:

  • Device manufacturer, model, and operating system version
  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad frequency capping and analytics
  • General, non-precise location (such as country or region)
  • In-game ad performance metrics (ad impressions, video completion)

For more details on how Unity Ads processes data, please visit Unity Ads Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Crowd Rush does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The ads served conform to Google Play Family Policy guidelines.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because your game save data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You may delete all save data at any time through the in-game Settings menu ("Reset Progress") or by clearing the app's cache and data in your device settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
